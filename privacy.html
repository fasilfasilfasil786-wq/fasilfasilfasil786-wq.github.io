Job Assistant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Job Assistant is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Muhammed Fasil, who uses it with his own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read): job-post emails the owner sends to his own address, and his Sent folder and replies, to keep his job-application tracker up to date.
  • Gmail (compose): to create email drafts. It never sends email; the owner reviews and sends each draft himself.
  • Google Drive (only files it creates): to save copies of the owner's tracker, CVs and cover letters. It cannot see any other Drive file.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to prepare the owner's own job applications. It is stored in the owner's private repository and his own Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
